World café

Four tables, about 15 minutes each. Hosts stay at their table; groups rotate. Add your answers to the Padlet from your phone: scan the QR code on the table or use the links in the table.

Table Prompt Examples On the Padlet
Data The weirdest or messiest dataset you've worked with. Satellite imagery with cloud cover over every scene; GPS traces with gaps; geotagged social media posts; admin boundaries that change every census; surveys or social statistics with inconsistent coding. Word cloud of dataset types, plus a 'messiest' ranking. Padlet
Place A place your research has taken you, physically or digitally. More than one is welcome! Fieldwork in Beirut; flood maps of Karachi seen from space; mobility data across Ukraine; Liverpool neighbourhoods on Street View. Pins on a world map. Padlet
Method A method you wish you knew, and one you could teach. Causal inference (difference-in-differences, regression discontinuity); spatial econometrics; sensitivity analysis; geospatial machine learning; remote sensing classification; network analysis. Two columns, 'want' and 'teach', to show matches. Padlet
Problem The question you'd work on with unlimited time and money, in 5 words max. "Where do displaced people go?" · "Can satellites measure poverty?" · "Who suffers most from heat?" A board of short questions, upvoted at the end. Padlet
